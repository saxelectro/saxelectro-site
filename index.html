Saxelectro Project Live · Semi Improvisation 5

The saxophone as a controller

Live audiovisual sets where the saxophone drives generative sound, composition, improvisation and visuals in real time.

The project is not about showing off saxophone technique. It looks for other ways to shape the saxophone's sound, to build new soundscapes, and to use the instrument itself as a controller on stage. Soprano, alto, tenor and baritone run through live electronics, sensors and real-time visuals.

Semi Improvisation means the set has a fixed structure and prepared material, but Andreas reshapes it in real time: he reorders the musical sections to suit the moment and turns the acoustic sound into something new as he plays.

Projects · 2010 to now

Project index

Each project gives the saxophone, a sensor, a camera or a metronome a different job on stage.

  • Unreal Engine · livestreamed

    Bridgection

    A 3D cinematic audiovisual piece that runs entirely in Unreal Engine. The name joins bridge and injection: a bridge where paradoxical, interactive events break into a simple scene. Kept live-only on purpose.

  • Unreal · Resolume · NDI

    Floating Islands

    A low-poly world for a two-stage show. Visuals generated from the processed sax in Max/MSP pass through Resolume and NDI into Unreal, where they become the materials on the main wall and the floating spheres.

  • Unreal · virtual camera

    The Night Room

    A room in a virtual house, built for a two-stage performance. Real-time visuals land on surfaces around the room while Andreas steers a virtual camera to reveal the second stage.

  • LPM 2018 · Rome

    Saxophonicly Electrosonics

    Audio-reactive generative visuals driven by the processed saxophone and the performer's body, with rhythms built from the deconstructed sax sound.

  • LPM XIII · Brancaleone, Rome · 2013

    Moving Heads

    The movement of the saxophone controls the musical events, the sound, the visual effects and the speed of the video.

  • Analog meets digital

    Metronomica

    Analog metronomes and other rhythm instruments dictate everything in a digital audiovisual performance.

  • Piezo triggers

    Drumelectro

    Open e-drumming: processed drum sound with live electronics, where piezo pickups turn every hit into sound or into data that triggers events.

  • Syntagma Metro Station, Athens · 2014

    Christmas Play

    Christmas ornaments and toys played under a live capture camera, turned into real-time visuals. With Katerina Kyriazopoulou, for the City of Athens.

  • Sensors

    Misty · To the War

    Short pieces where a Bluetooth sensor triggers sounds or a hand sensor drives the music and a slide show, while the sax improvises over them.

Saxelectro devices · Max for Live · since 2010

Tools built for the sax

More than 30 Max for Live devices that Andreas wrote for his own shows. Each one turns something on stage (a note, a colour, a movement, a hand clap) into a value that can control anything in Ableton Live or beyond.

  • Sax Audio FFT

    Play a note, trigger an event

    Listens to the saxophone, tracks pitch in hertz, and fires MIDI or maps parameters when a chosen note is played. Began as the 2010 patch Sax Hands, driven by hand claps on piezo sensors.

  • Sax Colors

    Image analysers

    Reads the red, green and blue levels of a video or live camera and maps them to any parameter on a 0–127 scale.

  • Sax Camera Control

    Video tracking

    Click any object in the preview and it is tracked in X and Y, ready to map.

  • Sax Blue

    Bluetooth sensors

    Connects two motion sensors at once: accelerometer X, Y, Z, buttons, joystick and battery, all mappable.

  • Sax Video Mapping

    Projection mapping

    Reshapes any video stream to fit the surface it is projected on, with as many instances as the GPU allows.

  • Sax Filter Res~

    Filters

    A very high-Q band-pass filter with a noise generator that can filter only the noise, making brand-new sounds.

  • Sax Glitchy Buffer

    Recorders and loopers

    Auto-record when sound arrives, then loop, speed-loop, chop and stutter, synced to Live or free.

  • Sax Many Knob · Sax Scenes

    Controllers

    Drive six devices from one knob, automate ramps, and fire Ableton scenes from sensors on the saxophone.

  • Tricky Chimes

    Ableton instrument

    Built from one close-miked A♭4 tubular bell, recorded in the Athens Municipal Symphony Orchestra's rehearsal hall, room noise included.

Teaching since 1992

Jazz Online Lessons

Live, interactive jazz improvisation classes for any instrument or voice, streamed from the studio since December 2015.

Each class runs 90 minutes. Students ask questions in the chat or join on air, and guest Greek jazz soloists give their own masterclasses. The first cycle covered theory and harmony, from the major scale and its chords to how scales and chords meet in improvisation. The next cycle moves on to repertoire analysis.

Seminar: The Technology in Music and Performing Arts

Run since 2013 in conservatories, high schools and universities, and with the City of Athens. Students take part in the live examples.

  1. 1Electronic music productionSetting up a home studio; software for composing, recording, producing and notation.
  2. 2Interactive multimediaBringing video, lighting, phones, tablets, Wi-Fi controllers and IP cameras into a musician's work.

About Andreas Mourtzoukos

A jazz and classical saxophonist who walked into electronics in 2000 and never walked back out.

Andreas studied classical and jazz saxophone, theory, composition, orchestration and improvisation at the Philippos Nakas, Athens, Athenaeum and Patras Municipal conservatories, then creative media, electronic music production, Max/MSP and Ableton Live at SAE Athens. He went on to Epic Games' professional game development programme and many more Unreal Engine courses in animation, level design, game audio, virtual production and cinematics.

He is a founding member of the Athens Big Band, has played with the National Symphony Orchestra of Greece and the Athens Municipal Symphony Orchestra, and was the first Greek saxophonist to play on the stage of the Ancient Theatre of Epidaurus, in Sophocles' Oedipus at Colonus (dir. Stavros Tsakiris).

Since 2013 he has toured schools, conservatories and universities with the seminar The Technology in Music and Performing Arts, and since 2014 he has broadcast his own weekly live streams: Saxelectro Live Sessions, Jazz Online Lessons and Saxelectro Private Concerts.

Full biography

Andreas Mourtzoukos: crossover saxophonist, audiovisual 3D artist. SAXELECTRO is his official artist name, and it runs through all of his projects and activities.

Andreas studied classical saxophone, jazz saxophone, music theory, art history, composition, orchestration, jazz improvisation and contemporary music performance at the Philippos Nakas Conservatory, the Athens Conservatory, the Athenaeum Conservatory and the Municipal Conservatory of Patras.

He also studied Creative Media, Electronic Music Production, Orchestration & Music Creation, Max/MSP and Ableton Live (1st prize) at SAE Athens International Institute. He completed Game Development with Unreal Engine, Epic Games' official professional programme on Coursera, along with many Unreal Engine courses on Udemy covering animation, level design, game mechanics, game audio, virtual production and cinematics.

Andreas has taught saxophone (classical and jazz), music theory and harmony, and the theory and practice of improvisation at the Philippos Nakas Conservatory in Athens, the Municipal Conservatory of Patras, the Municipal Music Centre of Piraeus and the International Course for Brass and Woodwinds in Kalavryta.

In classical and jazz music he has worked with major orchestras such as the National Symphony Orchestra of Greece, the Athens Municipal Symphony Orchestra and the National Radio & Television Orchestra of Greece. He is a founding member of the Municipal Big Band of Athens, known as the Athens Big Band, today one of the finest jazz orchestras in Europe. He has appeared at countless jazz festivals and venues in Greece and abroad as leader and sideman, sharing the stage with Bobby Shew, Ralph Peterson, Nils Landgren, Rex Richardson, Craig Bailey, Chico Freeman, Jean-Loup Longnon, Lana Janjanin, Reiner Witzel and others. He contributes to the concerts of the Greek Composers' Union, writes music for theatre, records music for cinema, and gives concerts in Greece and abroad.

As a musician and actor he has worked in numerous plays in Athens and abroad, and he is the first Greek saxophonist to play on the stage of the Ancient Theatre of Epidaurus, in Sophocles' Oedipus at Colonus, directed by Stavros Tsakiris.

2000As a saxophonist he entered the field of electronic music, both in education, presenting electroacoustic concerts at the Ionian University and elsewhere, and in the entertainment and music industry, collaborating with well-known electronica producers. He works with DJs, dancers and other artists from around the world in house music and other styles, bringing strong freestyle sax performances to major clubs, venues and arenas in Greece and abroad.

2010He presents his music through the concept SAXELECTRO PROJECT LIVE SEMI IMPROVISATION 5, a project that combines the sound and movement of the saxophones with electronic music technology, interactive multimedia and visuals, moving from experimental music creation into real music entertainment.

2013He created the educational seminar programme The Technology in Music and Performing Arts, which explores today's technological achievements and how they can give a modern view of music, theatre and creative media. It runs in Greek conservatories, high schools, universities and other venues, and remains very popular because the students become part of the examples and take a role in the presentation.

2014Andreas Mourtzoukos aka Saxelectro became the first Greek artist to present his work personally, without outside support, on a weekly basis via live streaming. His own internet broadcasts present his artwork and long experience in music and education in three directions: Saxelectro Live Sessions, Jazz Online Lessons and Saxelectro Private Concerts.

2015He has worked as a digital scenographer, using his skills in cross-media performing arts, digital design and theatre. With his own cross-media setup for big-screen projection and video mapping, he designs his own creations for concerts, theatrical performances and operas.

2016He has worked as digital set designer and video artist on plays and operas of the international repertoire, such as Jacques Offenbach's La Belle Hélène (dir. Isidoros Sideris), William Shakespeare's A Midsummer Night's Dream (dir. Vassilis Nikolaidis) and Edvard Grieg's Peer Gynt (dir. Vassilis Nikolaidis).

2020He has focused on Unreal Engine, the most complete and efficient video game and audio development software. Coming from ten years of hard and very creative work with Max/MSP, Andreas finds Unreal Engine very familiar to program, thanks to visual scripting, an efficient way of programming on both platforms. Taking many courses and practising many hours a day, he plans to bring all of his knowledge and experience in music, visuals and performing arts into new expandable 3D worlds, audiovisual experiences, live performances and games. Of course, music and the saxophones take first place on this journey.

Andreas also works with Isotonik Studios, Amazing Noises, Soundmanufacture, k-teck, Dillon Bastan, Patches Zone and others as a beta tester for Max for Live devices.

Ξεκίνησε την ενασχόλησή του με τη μουσική σε μικρή ηλικία, αρχικά με τα Ντραμς, και αργότερα με το Σαξόφωνο, που ήταν και η βάση της μουσικής του καριέρας και από το 1990 τον έχει οδηγήσει σε όλες τις διαδρομές και τις εμπειρίες ενός Σολίστ του Σαξοφώνου. Από το 1992, με αφετηρία το Κεντρικό Ωδείο Κώστα Κλάβα, δίδαξε Κλασσικό και Jazz Σαξόφωνο καθώς και Κλασσικά και Jazz θεωρητικά της Μουσικής στο Ωδείο Φίλιππος Νάκας (Αθήνα, Πάτρα), στο Δημοτικό Ωδείο Πάτρας, στο Πρότυπο Μουσικό Κέντρο Πειραιά, στο Σύγχρονο Ωδείο Αθήνας, καθώς και σε αρκετά Σεμινάρια Σαξοφώνου και Jazz Αυτοσχεδιασμού, όπως το Διεθνές Σεμινάριο Πνευστών στα Καλάβρυτα.

Προερχόμενος από 10 χρόνια σκληρής και πολύ δημιουργικής εργασίας με το Max/MSP, βρίσκει την Unreal Engine πολύ οικεία στον τρόπο προγραμματισμού, χάρη στο Visual Scripting, μια πολύ αποτελεσματική γλώσσα προγραμματισμού και για τις δύο πλατφόρμες. Παρακολουθώντας πολλά εκπαιδευτικά μαθήματα και εξασκούμενος πολλές ώρες την ημέρα, σχεδιάζει να αξιοποιήσει όλες τις γνώσεις και την εμπειρία του στη Μουσική, τα Εικαστικά και τις Παραστατικές Τέχνες, δημιουργώντας νέους επεκτάσιμους τρισδιάστατους κόσμους, οπτικοακουστικές εμπειρίες, ζωντανές εμφανίσεις και παιχνίδια.
